**Project:** 10-Gbps All-Digital True Random Number Generator (TRNG)
**Process:** TSMC 28HPC+
**Role:** Led chip tape-out (Sep 2021)

An all-digital true random number generator based on an ML-resistant middle
square method, targeting high-throughput hardware security. Results were
published in **A-SSCC 2022** and **IEEE JSSC 2024**.
