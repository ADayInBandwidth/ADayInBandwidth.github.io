**Project:** Full-Custom SRAM for High-Speed ADCs
**Process:** Samsung 28LPP (28 nm Low-Power Plus)
**Role:** Led full-custom design and tape-out (Sep 2020)

Designed and taped out a full-custom SRAM macro used as on-chip memory for
high-speed ADC data capture. Work covered bitcell/array design, sense
amplifiers, and timing, delivered as a full-custom layout.
